I’ve always loved staying active, and cycling is my absolute favorite. There’s something special about the mix of endurance, focus, and pure freedom you get on the bike—nothing else quite compares. Long rides help me clear my mind while keeping me in great shape.

Besides cycling, calisthenics is my go-to for building strength. I love that it’s all about mastering your own body weight. Over time I’ve worked up to 20 strict pull-ups and 20 kg weighted pull-ups for 12 reps — it’s tougher than it sounds!

(Sorry, not many bike photos — I’m really not into stopping to take pictures, haha.)

I also make time for friends and group sports, even if they’re not my main thing. Those shared moments build real connections and great memories. Fitness isn’t just personal goals for me — it’s about the people and experiences along the way.

Good nutrition matters too. After hard rides or workouts I focus on balanced recovery meals that fuel me right and keep my energy up. Food is fuel, but it’s also part of feeling good overall.

I used to play an instrument, but school and training have pushed it aside for now. I’ll definitely get back to it when life gives me the space — balance is important.

In the end, whether it’s pushing limits on the bike, getting stronger, hanging with friends, or just enjoying good food — it all helps me stay balanced and happy. Life is about enjoying the journey, not just the milestones.
